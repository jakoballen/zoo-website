Welcome

Welcome to a place where wildlife, education, and conservation come together. The Henry Vilas Zoo is home to animals from around the world and gives visitors the chance to learn more about the natural world in an exciting and memorable way.

Whether you are visiting to see your favorite animals, explore beautiful habitats, or learn about conservation efforts, there is something for everyone to enjoy.

Explore, Learn, and Connect

Our zoo encourages guests to connect with wildlife and better understand the importance of protecting animals and their habitats. From large mammals to colorful birds and reptiles, each exhibit helps tell a larger story about life on Earth.

Plan Your Visit

Use this website to join our newsletter, meet some of the animals, and contact us with questions about your upcoming visit. We hope your time at the zoo inspires curiosity, appreciation, and care for the natural world.
